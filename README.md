# 📱 Auto Ringer

### Automatically switch your phone between Ring and Silent mode based on where you are.

Ever reached college and realized your phone was still on Ring?  
Or reached home and missed an important call because you forgot to switch it back?

**Auto Ringer** solves that automatically.

The app uses Android geofencing to detect when you enter or leave saved locations and changes your phone's sound mode accordingly.

---

## ✨ What It Does

Auto Ringer lets you save two locations:

- 🏠 **Home**
- 🎓 **College**

Once automatic switching is enabled:

| Location/Event | Phone Mode |
|---|---|
| Enter College | 🔕 Silent |
| Leave College | 🔔 Ring |
| Enter Home | 🔔 Ring |

So instead of remembering to change your phone's mode manually, **your location does it for you.**

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📍 Location-Based Automation
Uses Android's geofencing capabilities to detect entry and exit from saved locations.

### 🔔 Automatic Sound Switching
Automatically switches between Ring and Silent mode depending on the current zone.

### 🏠 Custom Home & College Locations
Save your current location as Home or College directly from the app.

### 🎯 Adjustable Geofence Radius
Choose the radius used for detecting location changes.

Default: **150 meters**

Adjustable between **100–1000 meters**.

### 🔋 Battery Optimization Support
Includes battery optimization guidance to improve reliability on devices that aggressively restrict background apps.

### 🔄 Survives Phone Restart
The app re-registers its automation after the device is restarted.

### 🧪 Manual Testing
Test Ring and Silent mode without physically travelling between locations.
